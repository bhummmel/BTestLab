# NAS & Local AI Platform Design

## Overview

The NAS & Local AI Platform Design project focused on designing a unified infrastructure platform capable of simultaneously supporting high-capacity storage services and local artificial intelligence workloads.

The environment combines network-attached storage, GPU acceleration, virtualization, containerized services and self-hosted AI platforms in order to create a centralized infrastructure capable of supporting both traditional homelab workloads and emerging AI applications.

The project emphasizes infrastructure consolidation, operational efficiency, local data ownership and scalable computing resources while providing practical experience with systems architecture, storage engineering and AI infrastructure planning.

---

## Objectives

The primary objective of the project was to design a platform capable of serving as both a storage appliance and an AI compute environment.

The architecture was designed to support:

* centralized storage
* virtualization workloads
* containerized services
* local AI inference
* automation infrastructure
* media services

The project also served as an opportunity to evaluate the operational requirements of combining storage and compute-intensive AI workloads within a single infrastructure platform.

The environment prioritizes flexibility, scalability and long-term maintainability.
