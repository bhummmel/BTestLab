# TrueNAS Recovery & Migration

## Overview

The TrueNAS Recovery & Migration project focused on recovering critical homelab infrastructure following storage and platform instability while successfully migrating services, applications and operational workloads to a new infrastructure environment.

The project involved service recovery, storage troubleshooting, configuration preservation, data migration and platform redesign in order to restore infrastructure functionality while improving long-term reliability and maintainability.

The migration process provided practical experience with disaster recovery planning, service restoration, storage management and infrastructure modernization.

The environment emphasized operational continuity, data preservation and infrastructure resilience throughout the recovery and migration process.

---

## Objectives

The primary objective of the project was to recover critical services and data while minimizing downtime and preserving operational continuity.

Infrastructure migration was performed in order to improve platform stability, simplify management and provide a more sustainable long-term hosting environment.

The project focused on preserving application data, service configurations and operational workflows while transitioning infrastructure to a new platform.

The migration also served as an opportunity to evaluate infrastructure design decisions, identify operational weaknesses and improve recoverability.

The project provided valuable experience with large-scale service migration, infrastructure planning and disaster recovery procedures.

## Migration Strategy

The migration process was designed to minimize downtime while preserving service functionality and configuration consistency.

Applications and services were migrated in stages rather than through a single large-scale cutover.

This approach allowed validation of individual workloads before moving additional services.

Migration planning focused on:

* service continuity
* configuration retention
* data preservation
* workload validation
* dependency management

The staged migration methodology reduced risk and simplified troubleshooting throughout the transition process.
