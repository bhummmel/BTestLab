# TrueNAS ZFS Recovery & Homelab Maintenance

## Overview

The TrueNAS ZFS Recovery & Homelab Maintenance project focused on diagnosing, recovering and stabilizing a self-hosted infrastructure environment following storage-related failures, service interruptions and system maintenance events.

The project involved troubleshooting storage pool issues, recovering critical infrastructure services, validating data integrity and implementing maintenance procedures designed to improve long-term reliability and operational resilience.

The environment provided practical experience with ZFS administration, storage troubleshooting, infrastructure recovery planning and long-term homelab maintenance strategies.

---

## Objectives

The primary objective of the project was to recover infrastructure services while preserving data integrity and minimizing downtime.

Storage recovery procedures were performed in order to restore service availability and validate the health of the underlying storage infrastructure.

Infrastructure maintenance activities were used to improve long-term reliability, operational consistency and recoverability across the homelab environment.

The project also served as an opportunity to gain practical experience with ZFS administration, storage diagnostics, capacity planning and recovery workflows.

The environment was designed to prioritize data protection, service continuity and infrastructure stability throughout the recovery process.
