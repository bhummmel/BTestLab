# GPU Inference Hardware Evaluation

## Overview

The GPU Inference Hardware Evaluation project focused on researching, benchmarking and comparing hardware platforms capable of supporting local artificial intelligence workloads within a self-hosted infrastructure environment.

The project involved evaluating consumer GPUs, enterprise accelerators, workstation hardware, embedded AI platforms and supporting infrastructure requirements in order to determine the most effective solutions for local AI inferencing, voice assistants, computer vision and automation workloads.

The environment prioritizes local processing, privacy, operational flexibility and cost efficiency while providing practical experience with AI infrastructure planning and hardware selection.

---

## Objectives

The primary objective of the project was to identify hardware capable of efficiently supporting local AI workloads without reliance on cloud-based services.

The evaluation focused on:

* AI inferencing performance
* VRAM capacity
* power efficiency
* infrastructure compatibility
* acquisition cost
* long-term scalability

The project also served as an opportunity to understand the relationship between hardware specifications and real-world AI performance.

The environment was designed to support informed infrastructure purchasing decisions rather than relying solely on marketing specifications or benchmark data.

VRAM availability became a major decision factor when evaluating hardware options.

## Lessons Learned

VRAM capacity is often more important than raw processing performance for local AI workloads.

Consumer GPUs frequently offer excellent value for self-hosted AI environments.

Enterprise hardware may provide advantages in specific scenarios but often introduces additional deployment complexity.

Infrastructure compatibility is as important as benchmark performance when selecting AI hardware.

Local AI workloads benefit significantly from careful planning and capacity forecasting.

Technology evaluation should focus on operational requirements rather than specifications alone.
