# Switch Deployment & PoE Planning

## Overview

The Switch Deployment & PoE Planning project focused on designing, deploying and managing a scalable network switching environment capable of supporting self-hosted infrastructure, wireless networking, automation systems, surveillance devices and future expansion requirements.

The project involved switch evaluation, deployment planning, Power over Ethernet (PoE) capacity analysis, device onboarding and infrastructure growth forecasting in order to establish a reliable networking foundation.

The environment combines managed switching infrastructure, centralized management platforms and structured deployment methodologies while providing practical experience with enterprise networking concepts and infrastructure planning.

---

## Objectives

The primary objective of the project was to deploy a managed switching environment capable of supporting both current infrastructure requirements and future expansion.

Power over Ethernet planning was incorporated to simplify device deployment while reducing dependency on separate power infrastructure.

The project focused on improving:

* network scalability
* infrastructure visibility
* deployment flexibility
* operational reliability
* centralized management

The project also served as an opportunity to gain practical experience with switch administration, capacity planning, PoE budgeting and enterprise networking practices.

## Power over Ethernet Strategy

PoE planning was a major component of the project.

Power over Ethernet simplifies deployment by allowing compatible devices to receive both data connectivity and electrical power through a single network cable.

The environment was designed to support devices such as:

* wireless access points
* automation hardware
* voice infrastructure
* monitoring devices
* future network appliances

PoE deployment reduces infrastructure complexity and improves flexibility when adding new devices.

The project provided practical experience with power budgeting and infrastructure planning.
